Worked for 12 weeks under the supervision of [Kevin Resch](https://services.iqc.uwaterloo.ca/people/profile/kresch/) on the Quantum Oprics and Quantum Information of the Institute for Quantum Computing at the University of Waterloo. 

My work consisted in helping to set up and align an optical device for quantum state tomography. This involved an extensive literature review, running and developing simulations of the experiment and creating automation pipelines for some parts of the experiment.
